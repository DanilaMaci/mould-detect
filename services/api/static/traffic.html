How to read this

Requests / s, errors, latency, in flight are the four golden signals (traffic, errors, latency, saturation). p50 is the typical request; p99 is the slowest 1 % — it rises first when the server starts to queue. When requests arrive faster than they are answered, in flight climbs to the limit (--max-in-flight) and further requests are shed with 503 at once instead of waiting until they time out. Prometheus reads the same counters from /metrics.
